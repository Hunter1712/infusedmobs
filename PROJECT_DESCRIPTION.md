# Infused Mobs

> **Hostile mobs spawn with occult-infused tiers and randomized abilities — fully configurable via JSON.**

**Infused Mobs** makes every hostile encounter a gamble. Vanilla monsters spawn with one of three occult tiers and draw powers from a shared pool of 14 abilities — a poison hit here, a speed buff there, a death explosion when you least want one. Infused mobs are common, but their individual effects are kept deliberately weak, and everything is tunable.

## Tiers

- 🟢 **Cinder** — 40% of hostile spawns · 1 ability · 1.5× health & XP · green nametag
- 🟡 **Shade** — 20% of hostile spawns · 2 abilities · 2× health & XP · yellow nametag
- 🔴 **Doom** — 10% of hostile spawns · 3 abilities · 4× health & XP · red nametag

Tiers roll as a single uniform decision, so at default settings about **30% of hostile mobs stay vanilla**. Abilities range from damage-over-time and passive buffs to death-time splitting and explosions.

## Requirements

- **Fabric Loader 0.19.3+** and **Fabric API**
- Minecraft **1.20.1** (Java 17+), **1.21.1** (Java 21+) or **26.2** (Java 25+) — download the jar whose `+mc` suffix matches your game (e.g. `infusedmobs-2.8.0+1.21.1.jar`)
- Install on both client and server

## Installation

1. Install Fabric Loader and Fabric API for your Minecraft version.
2. Download the jar matching your Minecraft version.
3. Place it in your `mods/` folder and launch.
4. The config generates at `config/infusedmobs.json` on first run.
